These terms cover the beta workspace at [alicein.app/start](https://alicein.app/start). They are between you and Alice.In, the people who operate alicein.app. If you use it for a company, "you" includes that company.

Last updated 6 October 2026.

## The beta

The product is in beta. It can break, change, or be down. We can end a workspace, or the beta, with notice to the email on the account. You can leave at any time by asking us to export or delete the workspace.

There is no uptime promise and no support hours. We answer [hi@alice.in](mailto:hi@alice.in) when we can.

## Your work

You own the record in your workspace. You are responsible for having the right to put it there. If the work belongs to an employer, you need their permission before it goes in.

One Google account is one workspace. Signing in does not give your teammates access.

## Our side

We host the workspace, show it back to you and to the agent you connect, and answer in-app chat from it. We do not use it to train a model.

We may open a workspace to keep the service running or to help you. We describe where it lives and who else touches it on the [Security](/security) page.

## Leaving

Ask us and we send a copy of the workspace, remove the live copy, or both. A host snapshot can remain for up to five days. Details are in [How we handle the workspace](/data-processing).

## Liability

The beta is provided as it is. We are not liable for lost work, a missed snapshot, or a decision you or your agent make from the record. Nothing here limits liability that the law does not allow us to limit.
