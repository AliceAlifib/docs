You're already signed up. Here's how to actually use Alice.In.

## 1. Open your workspace

Launch the Alice.In app. You'll land in your 3D project graph — your **purpose** is at the center, with **goals**, **milestones**, **enablers**, and **tasks** branching out from it.

If the graph is empty, that's fine. Brain will help you fill it.

## 2. Talk to Brain

Brain is your AI co-founder. She knows your full project — every decision logged, every task tracked, every milestone hit or missed.

Just type. Examples:

- *"What's the next thing I should work on?"*
- *"Log a decision: we're using Postgres, not Mongo."*
- *"Add a milestone for closed beta by June."*
- *"What did I decide about auth last week?"*

She'll read the graph, update it when you ask, and push back when something doesn't make sense.

## 3. Switch how she thinks

Two quick controls:

- **Model**: `/model flash` (fast) or `/model pro` (deeper reasoning)
- **Tone**: `/set-tone direct`, `/set-tone energized`, or `/set-tone coach`

See [Commands](/guides/commands) for the full list.

## 4. Spawn a ClawBot

ClawBots are role-specific sub-agents. Use them when you want a focused operator instead of Brain herself.

```
/configure-agent
```

Brain will interview you and generate the configs for the new bot.

More: [ClawBots](/concepts/clawbots).
