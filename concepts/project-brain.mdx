**Brain** is the AI that runs alongside your project in Alice.In. Not a chatbot — a co-founder.

## What she knows

- Your full project graph: goals, milestones, enablers, tasks
- Your decision log — every architectural and product call you've made
- Your flight log — the running record of what's happened
- Her own memory — notes she writes to herself across sessions

## What she does

- **Tracks state.** She reads the live project structure before answering, so she's never guessing.
- **Pushes execution.** Asks what's blocking. Suggests the next move. Flags drift.
- **Has opinions.** She'll disagree if she sees a better path. That's the point.
- **Manages sub-agents.** She can spawn and direct ClawBots for focused work.

## What she won't do

- Hold credentials. Zero secrets, ever.
- Delete things. Soft-delete only — your history stays intact.
- Take big external actions without your approval.

## How she stays current

Each session she wakes up fresh and re-reads her workspace files. Those files **are** her memory. When she learns something worth keeping, she writes it down so the next session inherits it.
