A **sprite** is the live, persistent representation of an agent and its workspace inside Alice.In. When you see Brain in the 3D graph, you're looking at her sprite.

## What's in a sprite

- The agent's configs (identity, soul, tools, memory)
- Its working state and logs
- Its position in your workspace graph

## Why sprites matter

Sprites are why Brain has continuity. The sprite **is** the agent — its files, its memory, its history — all in one addressable place. Edits you make to the sprite are persistent. Edits the agent makes to herself are persistent too.

## The graph

Your workspace is a graph of sprites and project nodes:

- **Purpose** at the center
- **Goals** branching out
- **Milestones** under goals
- **Enablers** under milestones
- **Tasks** under enablers
- Sprites (Brain, ClawBots) living alongside the structure

Everything is editable. Drag, rename, restructure. Brain reads the graph as the source of truth.
