**Needs you** is the list of calls only you can make. If it is not here, it is not waiting on you.

## What lands here

- **Decisions to ratify** — your agent proposes a change to a plan or a decision; you approve or reject it with one tap. Nothing structural changes until you do. Open questions written into a living plan land here the same way.
- **Delivered work** — a card your agent finished and handed back. Open it, read it, check it off. Delivered is not done; done is your call.
- **Questions** — your agent asked you something and is holding until you answer.
- **Pull requests** — open PRs with their diff, screenshots and an AI read of the change, mergeable in place.

Held notes — waiting on someone, gone quiet, still open — live on the **Threads** tab of the agent-queue panel, not in this list. Open the queue and click Threads. Age shows loudly there, on purpose.

## Where to find it

Needs you is a tab in the agent-queue panel, and a count in the project map's header line. Both read the same list, so the number never disagrees with itself.

Press `n` for Needs you, unless an enabler panel is open — there `n` makes a new card.

## How to work it

Take the top item, decide, move on. If the list is growing, you are being asked to arbitrate too much.
