---
title: "Welcome to Alice.In"
description: "Your plan, your system, and the record of the work — in one place your agents can use."
---

Alice.In is the map of your project and the memory of it. Your own coding agent connects to that record, starts each session already knowing where you are, and writes what it does back into it.

Three views of the same project:

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="The project map" icon="diagram-project" href="/using/project-map">
    Your plan as a place. Phases across the top, enablers you drag to re-parent, work lighting up as it happens.
  </Card>
  <Card title="The factory map" icon="cubes" href="/using/factory-map">
    Your architecture as a place. Off until you turn **Show factory tab** on in Settings.
  </Card>
  <Card title="The knowledge view" icon="book-open" href="/using/knowledge">
    Everything your project knows, and what led to what.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
