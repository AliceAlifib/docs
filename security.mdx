Alice.In is in beta. This page describes the workspace you get from [alicein.app/start](https://alicein.app/start). It is not a SOC 2 report. We do not have one.

## What we store

The workspace holds the project record your agent writes: the plan, what happened, decisions, research, and notes. Sign-in stores the Google account's email and name.

Your repositories stay on your computer. The agent reads them there. A file reaches us only if the agent writes it into the record.

We do not ask for your Claude, Codex, or Grok key. The install command on Connect contains a token for your workspace. Treat that command like a password.

We do not take audio. Voice controls are not part of the beta.

## Where it lives

The service runs on Fly.io, in the San Jose region. Two disks:

- The workspace itself, on the hub. As of 8 October 2026 that app is in the Alice.In Fly org.
- The account (Google identity and the token that opens the workspace), on the login app. That app is still on the older shared Fly org.

Both disks are encrypted at rest. The sites are served over HTTPS. Checked 8 October 2026: the workspace volume still reported `ENCRYPTED: true` after the move.

Fly takes a daily snapshot of each disk and keeps it for five days. That is a recovery copy, not a second live workspace. A failure can lose what was written after the last snapshot.

## Who else sees it

| Who | What they see |
|---|---|
| Google | Email and name, for sign-in. |
| Microsoft Azure OpenAI | A question you ask in the in-app chat, and the parts of the workspace used to answer it. |
| Your own coding agent | Whatever that agent fetches, under your account with Anthropic, OpenAI, or xAI. We do not send the workspace to those companies ourselves. |
| Alice.In | The people who operate the service can open a workspace to keep it running or to help you. |

We do not use a workspace to train a model.

## A company of a few people

One Google account is one workspace. There is no invite for a teammate yet. For a company beta, one person owns the workspace. That person should have the company's permission to put the work there.

## Export and deletion

Email [efidler@alicein.xyz](mailto:efidler@alicein.xyz). We send a copy of the workspace, or we remove it, or both. There is no button for this yet.

Removing it includes the daily snapshots, so the copy does not sit there for the rest of the five days.

## What this is not

No penetration test, no uptime promise, no completed security audit. The beta can be down, and it can change. The terms of that are in [Beta terms](/beta-terms). What we do with the workspace is in [How we handle the workspace](/data-processing).
