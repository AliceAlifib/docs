This is the note to give a company whose people use the beta. It is written in plain language. It is not a signed data-processing agreement. If your counsel needs a signature block, send it to [hi@alice.in](mailto:hi@alice.in) and we will put the legal name on it.

Last updated 6 October 2026.

## Who does what

You decide what goes into the workspace. We process it to provide the beta: store it, show it, let your agent read and write it, answer in-app chat, help you, and delete or export it when you ask.

Alice.In means the people who operate alicein.app and the hub at alicein-hub.fly.dev.

## What is in it

- Google email and name.
- The project record your agent writes.
- Questions you ask in the in-app chat.

Source code stays on your machines unless an agent writes it into the record. We do not collect audio.

Do not put passwords, access keys, or health data in the record. We do not scan for them. If one is written in, it is stored with the rest.

## Where, and who else

The disks are in Fly.io's San Jose region, encrypted at rest, served over HTTPS. Fly keeps a daily snapshot for five days.

| Subprocessor | Why |
|---|---|
| Fly.io | Hosts the machines and the disks. |
| Google | Sign-in. Receives email and name. |
| Microsoft Azure OpenAI | Writes the in-app chat answer from the question and the records used to answer it. |

Your coding agent runs on your own Claude, Codex, or Grok account. That provider sees what your agent sends them. That agreement is yours, not this one.

We do not sell the workspace. We do not use it to train a model.

## How long

We keep the live workspace until you ask us to delete it, or until the beta ends and we tell you. After a deletion, a snapshot can remain for up to five days.

## A team

The workspace belongs to one Google account. There is no member list. The company should name the person who may put company work there.

## Asking for something

Export, deletion, or a question: [hi@alice.in](mailto:hi@alice.in).
