---
title: "ClawBots"
description: "Sub-agents you spawn for specific roles."
---

A **ClawBot** is a focused sub-agent. Brain is your generalist co-founder; ClawBots are specialists.

## When to use one

- A recurring job with a clear scope (research, writing, code review, scraping)
- Work you want isolated from Brain's main context
- A role that needs its own personality, tools, or memory

## Creating one

In a session with Brain:

```
/configure-agent
```

Brain interviews you — what's the bot for, what tools does it need, what tone — then generates its config. Once configured, you can call it from your workspace.

## What lives inside a ClawBot

Same building blocks as Brain, scoped narrower:

- **Identity** — name, role, vibe
- **Soul** — beliefs, what it will and won't do
- **Tools** — what actions it can take
- **Memory** — its own notes, separate from Brain's

You own the configs. Tweak them anytime.
