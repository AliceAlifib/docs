title: "Alice (chat)"
description: "Ask your project questions and get cited answers."
---

Alice sits at the bottom right of every view. She answers from the project's record and cites what she used.

## What to ask

- *"Where are we?"*
- *"What needs me?"*
- *"Why did we decide to use Postgres?"*
- *"What happened on the payments work last week?"*
- *"What is this module for?"* — with the factory map open

If the record cannot answer, she says so. "We never wrote that down" tells you what to capture next.

## Controls

| Action | How |
|---|---|
| Send | `Enter` |
| Push to talk | Hold `Space` |
| Dictate on/off | `Ctrl`/`⌘` + `D` |
| Grow or shrink the panel | `e` |
| Close the panel | `Esc` |

Chat also pops out into its own window if you would rather keep it beside the map. Allow pop-ups for the page if the button does nothing.

The composer accepts a pasted screenshot (`Ctrl`/`⌘` + `V`) and files dragged in.

## Asking about a module

Open a module on the [factory map](/using/factory-map) and ask your question there. The answer is grounded in that module's real files and connections, and it shows you the receipts — which files and records it read.
