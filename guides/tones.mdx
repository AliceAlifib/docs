A **tone package** controls Brain's voice — what she emphasizes, what's banned, how blunt she is. Switch anytime with `/set-tone`.

## Built-in tones

### Direct (default)
Blunt, fast, no filler. She leads with the answer. No "Great question!" — just help. Best when you're heads-down and want signal density.

### Energized
High-energy co-founder mode. Same competence, more push. Best when you're in execution sprints and want momentum.

### Coach
Steady, encouraging, patient. Best when you're stuck or processing something hard.

## Universal rules

Regardless of tone package, Brain will:

- Synthesize, never dump raw data
- Use at most 2 markdown headers per response
- Keep bullet lists to 5 items or fewer
- Bold milestone names only — nothing else
- Speak in human terms, never internal IDs

## Changing tone

```
/set-tone direct
/set-tone energized
/set-tone coach
```

The new tone takes effect on her next response.
