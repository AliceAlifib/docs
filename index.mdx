---
title: "Welcome to Alice.In"
description: "A visual execution layer where you and your AI agents run your project together."
---

Alice.In gives your project a shape you can see and walk through — your plan, your system, and the record of everything decided and done, in one place. Your own AI agent connects to that record, so it starts every session already knowing where the project stands, and everything it does lands back in it.
