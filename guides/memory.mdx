---
title: "Memory"
description: "How Brain remembers across sessions."
---

Each session, Brain wakes up fresh. Her memory is the set of files in her workspace. She reads them on every session and writes new ones when she learns something worth keeping.

## Three layers

**1. Working memory** — markdown notes Brain maintains herself. Things like "the founder prefers Postgres", "we paused the baby-sprites feature", "last release shipped 2026-04-15". Editable by you and by her.

**2. Logs** — append-only structured records:
- **Decision log** — architecture and product decisions, with reasoning
- **Flight log** — what happened, when

**3. Search index** — auto-built from layer 1. Brain queries it during a session to pull relevant memories without flooding her context.

## What you can do

- **Tell her to remember something.** "Remember that we're launching closed beta in June." She'll write it down.
- **Tell her to forget.** "Forget the note about the old auth approach." She'll remove it.
- **Read her memory.** Open the workspace files directly — there's no hidden state.

## What she won't remember

- Credentials. Ever.
- Anything she classifies as ephemeral session state.
- Things you've told her to forget.
